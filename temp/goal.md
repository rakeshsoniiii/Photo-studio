# Studio Erato Films — Project Goals

## Primary Goal

Create a premium cinematic website for **Studio Erato Films** that turns the studio's photography and films into a luxury digital experience and helps convert visitors into qualified wedding enquiries.

The site should feel like watching the opening sequence of a wedding film rather than browsing a standard photography template.

---

## Business Goals

### 1. Generate More Qualified Enquiries
The website should guide users naturally toward:
- checking availability
- contacting the studio
- sharing wedding date/location
- starting a WhatsApp or enquiry conversation

The CTA should feel premium, not pushy.

### 2. Strengthen Premium Brand Positioning
Studio Erato should appear as a high-end destination wedding photography and filmmaking studio.

Key positioning to reinforce:
- Destination Weddings & Bespoke Films
- Documentary + Editorial approach
- Limited number of weddings
- Storytelling over mass-volume photography

### 3. Convert Instagram Attention Into Owned Traffic
Social media is useful for discovery, but the website should become the place where potential clients:
- understand the studio
- explore full wedding stories
- watch films
- see testimonials
- check availability
- contact the studio

### 4. Showcase Work Better Than Social Media
Instagram crops and compresses content.

The website should provide:
- full-width photography
- curated wedding stories
- cinematic films
- high-resolution editorial galleries
- destination-specific storytelling

### 5. Create a Memorable First Impression
Within the first 5–10 seconds, visitors should understand:
- this is a premium wedding studio
- the studio creates both photography and films
- the work has an editorial/cinematic identity
- the experience is intentionally crafted

---

## Experience Goals

The site should feel:

- cinematic
- luxurious
- emotional
- editorial
- handcrafted
- fast
- responsive
- effortless

It should not feel:

- like a template
- like a SaaS landing page
- like an agency portfolio
- like a generic photographer theme
- overloaded with effects

---

## Technical Goals

Build with:

- Next.js App Router
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- GSAP
- GSAP ScrollTrigger
- Lenis
- Embla Carousel where necessary
- Next/Image
- Cloudinary-ready media architecture

Code goals:
- modular
- clean
- reusable
- responsive
- accessible
- production-ready
- CMS-ready later

---

## Signature Interaction Goals

The first version should successfully deliver these high-impact interactions:

### 1. Curved Fullscreen Menu Transition
A black cinematic menu closes upward like a theatrical curtain.

### 2. Cinematic Hero
Asymmetric wedding collage with subtle parallax and large editorial typography.

### 3. Pinned Horizontal Photography Journey
Vertical page scrolling translates into a large horizontal editorial gallery.

### 4. Film Hover / Play Experience
Video cards feel cinematic and open into an elegant fullscreen player.

### 5. Diagonal Final CTA Marquees
Two opposing marquee bands create a memorable closing section.

These five interactions define the site's personality.

---

## Content Goals

Use real Studio Erato content wherever possible.

Never invent:
- awards
- publications
- client counts
- wedding counts beyond confirmed information
- destinations served
- testimonials
- partnerships
- brand associations

Use placeholders until real content is supplied.

---

## Conversion Goals

Primary CTA:
`CHECK AVAILABILITY`

Secondary CTAs:
- Explore Stories
- View Photography
- Watch Film
- Start Your Story
- Get in Touch

Avoid too many competing CTAs.

---

## SEO Goals

Build technical foundations for:
- Wedding Photographer
- Destination Wedding Photographer
- Wedding Films
- Wedding Photography Ranchi
- Wedding Photographer Jharkhand
- Destination Wedding Photography India

Do not keyword-stuff visible copy.

Add later:
- metadata
- Open Graph
- structured data
- sitemap
- robots
- descriptive URLs
- wedding story pages

---

## Mobile Goals

Most social-media traffic is likely to arrive on mobile.

The mobile site must:
- load quickly
- preserve premium feel
- avoid janky scroll effects
- use touch-first interactions
- simplify heavy desktop animation
- keep contact actions easy to reach

Mobile is not a secondary version.

---

## Performance Goals

Photography should look excellent without making the site unusable.

Targets:
- optimize hero assets
- lazy load below the fold
- AVIF/WebP
- image dimensions predeclared
- no unnecessary autoplay video
- avoid heavy JavaScript where CSS/native behavior works
- no layout shift from media
- smooth 60fps transforms on capable devices

---

## Accessibility Goal

The cinematic design should remain usable.

Must support:
- keyboard navigation
- readable contrast
- alt text
- reduced motion
- focus management
- accessible forms
- Escape-close modals and menu

---

## Definition of Success

The site is successful when a new visitor can:

1. Understand the Studio Erato brand within seconds.
2. Feel impressed by the visual experience without being confused.
3. Easily explore real wedding photography and films.
4. Learn what makes the studio different.
5. Contact the studio or check availability.
6. Enjoy the experience on both desktop and mobile.
7. Navigate without animation bugs or performance issues.
